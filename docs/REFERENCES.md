### Outcome

- **The landmarks are settled.** All seven Marina Bay landmarks liked (1 to 7). The views felt like repeats to him: "Most of the Marina bay just feels like repeats." No more rounds on which landmarks; the work is drawing them well.
- **The heartland is its landmark, not its texture.** Pinnacle@Duxton is the only like. Ordinary HDB blocks, the void deck and Lau Pa Sat were passed over, so ordinary blocks stay quiet massing.
- **No scan was picked, and that is the answer.** Rahul: the model was built from map data and then extruded by an algorithm, so he was not sure which scan would make the most sense. In Etavolt's words, LiDAR data taken from online map data and reconstructed into a rough mesh of the building ([CONTENT](CONTENT.md#2-etavolt--scanner)). So the city is not drawn in a lidar manner at all (no sensor rings, no colour by height). The chat's proposal from this, built as the fourth sketch: the city is built the way his model was, from the map up ([DECISIONS D39](DECISIONS.md)).
- **Easter eggs:** otters (25), a cat (27), the Supertree light show (28), the Changi control tower (29).
- Rahul also asked the chat not to be held by his picks and to think about the bigger picture. D39 is that.

## How to mark

The easy way: run `pnpm dev` and open `/workbench/references.html`. It shows every reference as a picture (loaded from Wikimedia, not stored here), with a Like and a Not this button on each and a button that copies your picks as text.

You do not pick one per group. The groups are only how the list is sorted. A handful you like, a couple you do not, and why, is enough.

Or mark this file directly: for each one, put `yes`, `no` or nothing in the Mark column, and a few words on why. What matters is the why: "the rings are the structure", "too clock-like", "like how dense it is". Add anything of your own at the bottom, including photos of things you have seen.

"Speaks to" is the part of the Machine the reference is most useful for. "Look at" is the quality worth judging, not a claim that the Machine should copy it.

## Outcome

Rahul's nine likes, 2026-10-06. The five words are the chat's reading of them. Rahul saw them and signed off a silhouette built from them ([DECISIONS D23](DECISIONS.md)).

- **Five words for the feeling:** futuristic, nested, wired, kinetic, dense.
- **Like this, as the whole object:** the quantum computer (tiered, hanging, makes you curious); the Connection Machine (a futuristic block); the fibre cable cut open (layers peeled back, fibres running through); Cassini (subsystems on one body).
- **Like this, as a part or a detail:** the gimbal's rings inside rings, for the Rings; the hard disk's sweeping arm, which Rahul likes but does not see as a whole machine; the chip die as a "machine city" on a surface; fibres as the routes between parts; the orrery's arms, because they promise good transitions; the turbofan, because it is familiar.
- **Not like this:** nothing was marked down. Passed over without comment: everything antique or ornate (armillary sphere, torquetum, Antikythera), loose wiring and patch panels, rows of racks, front panels, tape drives, heat sinks.
